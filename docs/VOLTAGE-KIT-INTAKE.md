## Visual thesis

Voltage is a vivid desktop instrument panel where every important state is a solid, touchable object. Bright color marks status and action; deep violet planes carry structure; generous rounded geometry makes operational software feel friendly without reducing legibility.

## Type, material, and motion

- Type: rounded system UI with durable fallbacks; compact labels remain firm and headings remain friendly.
- Material: solid planes, crisp borders, inset highlights, and directional seated depth. No glass or glow.
- Motion: short, property-specific state change only. No resting loop, floating lift, or decorative drift.

## Signature element

The Voltage instrument panel is a showroom proof and an optional composition, not a forced product layout. It demonstrates the rail, color-blocked status objects, health summary, and reversible task states while every adopting product keeps its own routes, content, data, behavior, and journeys.
