# Build Brand System

Build recognition from evidence and repeatable decisions, not decoration.

## Workflow

1. Read existing brand materials and product surfaces before proposing anything. Separate established facts from gaps.
2. Record the audience, category, job, alternative, difference, promise, proof, and constraints. Never invent testimonials, metrics, heritage, customers, or claims.
3. Create three genuinely different directions when the user has not already chosen one. Vary structure, typography, color roles, imagery, and behavior—not just the accent color.
4. Carry the chosen direction into a complete foundation:
   - positioning and message hierarchy;
   - vocabulary, cadence, preferred verbs, and refusal list;
   - display, body, and optional outlier type roles with licensing and fallbacks;
   - paper, ink, action, support, success, danger, and focus color roles in OKLCH;
   - wordmark, compact mark, favicon, clear space, and small-size behavior;
   - photography or illustration subjects, crops, treatment, and exclusions;
   - one structural or interactive signature people can recognize;
   - ownership, fixed rules, allowed variation, and exception handling.
5. Apply the system to representative product, campaign, content, and transactional surfaces so the rules are proven in use.
6. Test light, dark, mobile, accessibility, legibility, and difficult states before publishing the system.

## Quality bar

- Make the brand specific to its real nouns, verbs, audience, and context.
- Use no more than three type families on a surface.
- Use one accent for functional emphasis; tint neutrals toward the anchor hue.
- Do not use gradient text, generic emoji, glass, glow, ambient blobs, or stock AI illustration.
- Keep errors instructional and success quiet when the result is already visible.
- Preserve licenses and source provenance for every font and visual asset.

Deliver a working brand system and representative applications, not a mood board alone.
