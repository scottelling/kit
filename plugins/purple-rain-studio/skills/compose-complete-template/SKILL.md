# Compose Complete Template

A template is a working product system. Start with the decisions people need to make, then compose every surface required to complete and recover from the journey.

## Choose the family

- Launch: opening, product story, proof, plans or commitment, and questions.
- Product: overview, workspace, activity, settings, and recovery.
- Commerce: shop, collection, product, checkout, and receipt.
- Publication: edition, article, archive, search, and subscription.
- Portfolio: selected work, case study, approach, about, and contact.
- Documentation: start, guide, reference, examples, and changes.
- Marketplace: discovery, results, provider, booking or purchase, and account.
- Campaign: statement, story, participation, updates, and completion.

Use another family only when the actual user journey demands it. Name the family and its required surfaces before implementation.

## Compose the system

1. Define the primary journey, alternate journey, and recovery path.
2. Assign each page a single job, its required evidence, its primary action, and onward movement.
3. Establish shared navigation, page frames, content roles, controls, data patterns, feedback, and overlays.
4. Include loading, empty, error, disabled, selected, success, destructive, and permission states wherever the product can actually encounter them.
5. Use truthful representative content shaped around the product’s real nouns and verbs.
6. Make the pages share one visual and behavioral system while allowing each page to fit its job.
7. Verify keyboard movement, visible focus, 44-pixel touch targets, readable measure, reduced motion, and responsive recomposition.

## Quality bar

- Avoid the “component dump” pattern. Show complete decisions and flows.
- Avoid repeating the same hero, card grid, testimonial strip, and closing call to action on every family.
- Keep hierarchy structural: scale, placement, rules, spacing, and tonal planes before decorative effects.
- Treat mobile as a deliberate composition, not a shrunk desktop.
- Every template must be useful before customization and fully adjustable after selection.

Deliver the complete connected template, its difficult states, and a short receipt explaining what is included and why.
