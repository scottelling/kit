01

Governing rules

Six rules hold the system together. Every derived component below was built to obey them — that's what keeps the additions from reading as a second system bolted on.

RuleWhy it holds
Body copy is always greyHeadings and control labels take near-black, everything else takes the muted token. The contrast gap does the hierarchy work weight usually would.
One border width1px, one color. Structure comes from hairlines, not boxes or fills.
Flat by defaultShadow appears only where something genuinely floats — menus, overlays, the product mock. Never on cards or buttons.
Weight range is 400–500No bold anywhere, at any size. Emphasis is size and color.
Mono carries metadataSequence numbers, byte sizes, extensions, keys, section labels. Prose never uses it.
Pills for controls, 6–8px for surfacesButtons, badges, switches, avatars are fully round. Cards, inputs, menus, panels are not. The vocabularies never cross.

Dark theme derivationhow each value was reached
TokenDark valueReasoning
background10 10 10The source's own --surface. No new value invented — the dark page is their inverted section, scaled up.

Motion & elevation

Two durations, one curve. Shadow only where something genuinely floats.

TokenValueApplied to
duration-fast150msopacity and color on controls
duration500mscontent reveals, hover translate, progress fills
easecubic-bezier(0, 0, .2, 1)everything — ease-out only, no spring, no bounce
shadow-menu0 8px 24px -6px / 0 2px 6px -2pxderived — menus, palette, dialog, toast
shadow-float0 30px 70px -24pxextracted — the floating product mock, once
focus ring2px ring, offset 2pxevery interactive element, both themes

Dark-mode shadows do less work, not more. On a near-black page a drop shadow is nearly invisible, so overlay separation comes primarily from the hairline border — which is exactly why --border had to be lifted to #262626. The shadow opacities roughly double in dark to retain some depth cue, but they're no longer the load-bearing signal.

11

Remaining cautions

What's still unresolved after the extension.

IssueDetail
Google Sans isn't licensableGoogle's proprietary brand face — substitute before shipping. Geist, Inter, or Instrument Sans sit closest to its geometric-humanist proportions. Google Sans Code (the mono) is openly released and carries over intact.
Statement size is inferredThe pull quote resolved to 48.17px at a 1147px viewport. Its clamp is reconstructed from that ratio and its sibling declarations — verify at a second width if it matters.
No data visualizationThe storage meter is the only chart-like element here. A real charting layer needs a categorical sequence, and the palette gives you nothing to build one from without breaking the "color is functional only" rule.
Dark mode is unvalidated against the imageryThe site leans heavily on video and photo thumbnails designed against white. On near-black they'll read hotter and may need an inset hairline to sit properly.
Focus ring vs. dense rowsA 2px ring at 2px offset needs 4px of clearance. In the 30px sidebar and menu rows that eats the gap — either tighten the offset to 1px in dense contexts or accept slight overlap.
No responsive story for the browser shellThe sidebar/toolbar/row layout is desktop-shaped, matching the product. It stacks below 768px here, but a real collapse pattern has no precedent in the source.
